The world didn’t see it coming. After years of silence, Vega Nova dropped Echoes in Static , an album that feels like a pulse through the dark underbelly of modern sound. Blending brooding synth-wave, raw indie-electro beats, and haunting vocal layers, this record is a journey through neon-lit alleyways and midnight highways. Tracks like "Ghost Signal" and "Neon Ghosts" blend melancholy with rhythm so tight it sticks to your bones. The production is crisp but carries just enough grit to keep you feeling every ache and spark. It’s not just an album — it’s a mood, a moment, a mirror held up to the chaos of now. If 2025 had a soundtrack, this would be it.
